UUnitex

Install Unitex Helper

Browsers don't let one website read another website's pages, which is how your email and bank stay private from other sites. Unitex Helper is a tiny browser extension that loads search results from Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Brave, Ecosia and Mojeek in your own browser, with your cookies, so Unitex can show them all on one page. It can't load anything else, and only Unitex pages can use it.

Download Unitex Helper (.zip)

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You'll get a folder called unitex-helper.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right; in Edge it's on the left).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unitex-helper folder.
  5. Come back to this page and refresh. The box above turns green.

Firefox

  1. Download and unzip as above.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox and click Load Temporary Add-on….
  3. Choose manifest.json inside the unitex-helper folder.

Firefox removes add-ons loaded this way when it restarts, until Unitex Helper is published on Firefox Add-ons.

Phones

Mobile Chrome and Safari don't support extensions. On Android, Firefox, Kiwi, Yandex or Edge Canary can load extensions; on iPhone, Unitex works without the helper.

What it does, exactly

The whole extension is three short files you can read in extension/. When you search on Unitex, the page asks the helper for each engine's result page; the helper fetches it and hands back the HTML; Unitex picks out the results and shows each one with its website and the engines that found it. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
