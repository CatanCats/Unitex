# Unitex

One search box for the whole web: Unitex's own results, every major engine and AI one click away, topic filters that search all the sites at once, and news from everywhere.

**Live:** https://catancats.github.io/Unitex/

## Features

- **Unitex search** (`search.html`, the default) – Unitex shows results itself and never sends you off to another search site. Without Unitex Helper it only searches sources that let other websites read them: SearXNG public servers and Qwant, plus open web indexes that return actual websites (Mwmbl, Stract, Alexandria, Wiby, Marginalia) plus Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo instant answers, Reddit, Hacker News and the news feed and says clearly that it's only partly working; a "Searched" bar shows every source, what it returned, and which engines are locked until the helper is installed.
- **Real listings from realestate.com.au and Domain** – they let other websites read their search pages, so a Houses search in Australia reads them directly (no extension needed): Unitex finds the suburb, state and postcode on OpenStreetMap, loads both sites' results for rent or sale (and bedrooms), and shows each listing with its address, price, beds/baths/cars and photo, "found by realestate.com.au (read directly)". If a site sends a bot-check page instead, the "Searched" bar says so. Code in `listings.js`.
- **Tabs, with filters under each** – All · AI · Shopping · Images · Videos · News · Maps · Books, and More (Jobs, Travel, Code). Under a tab, filters narrow it: All → Recipes, Events, Movies & TV, Learn, Open source, Reviews; Videos → Movies & TV, Learn; Books → Free to read, Learn, Research papers; Code → Open source. (These replace the old "Looking for" row; old `?topic=` links still work.)
- **Shopping area** – the Shopping tab is a store-style area for every shop at once: departments (Products, Houses, Cars, Second-hand, Reviews), a filter sidebar (price, rating, new/used, store, has a price, sort by price/rating/most reviews), product cards with photo, price, rating and review count, condition, the store, "found by", **Buy at <store>** and ♡ Save, and **Your list** with a running total and a compare table. With Unitex Helper it reads Amazon, eBay (used items for Second-hand), Google Shopping, Bing Shopping and the big stores in your country (Australia: Kogan, Big W, Kmart, JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, The Good Guys, Harvey Norman; US: Walmart, Best Buy, Target; UK: Argos, Currys, John Lewis; NZ: PB Tech, Noel Leeming, The Warehouse; Canada: Best Buy, Walmart; second-hand: Gumtree, Trade Me, Kijiji). Store pages are read from the product data they carry (JSON-LD, Next.js data), so new stores are one line in `STORES` in `helper.js`; without it, products come from store pages found in search results. Houses read realestate.com.au and Domain directly in Australia (house cards with price, beds, baths, cars, buy/rent). Recipes (TheMealDB) and Movies & TV (TVmaze) get their own cards too. Code in `modes.js` and `helper.js` (`STORE_SOURCES`).
- **AI overview and AI tab** – every search on the All tab gets an AI overview. By default it's **Google's AI Overview**, read from Google's own results page (or Google's AI Mode) through Unitex Helper. A picker on the overview switches to Bing Copilot, Brave's AI summary, free online AI through [Puter](https://puter.com) (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Llama, Mistral; sign in to Puter once), online AI with your own API key (Claude Opus 5.5, ChatGPT, Gemini; keys stay in this browser and go only to that company), or AI on this computer (Chrome's Gemini Nano, or Llama/Qwen/Gemma through WebLLM). If the chosen one has no answer, Unitex tries the others by itself and says so. Online and on-computer answers are written from Unitex's results with numbered citations. The **AI** tab is a chat with the same choices, follow-ups, and "ask another AI too". Code in `ai.js`.
- **Settings** (⚙ in the header) – turn AI off (hides the overview and the AI tab), pick who writes the AI overview, add your own API keys, pick the country and theme, and switch all sources back on.
- **Tabs with their own experiences** (`tabs.js`):
  - **Videos** – SearXNG's video search (YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion… each labelled with the engine that found it), Qwant Videos, YouTube (through Invidious and Piped), PeerTube, Dailymotion, Odysee, Internet Archive, NASA, Commons, plus Google, Bing, Brave and SearXNG video pages with the helper. Videos play right in Unitex (YouTube via youtube-nocookie); filter by length, source, "plays here"; save to Watch later.
  - **Images** – SearXNG's image search (Google, Bing, Flickr, Unsplash… each labelled "Flickr Images (through SearXNG)"), Qwant Images, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, NASA, Art Institute of Chicago, Cleveland Museum of Art, Europeana and iNaturalist, plus Google, Bing, Brave, Mojeek, Yandex and SearXNG image pages with the helper. Full-screen viewer with next/previous, creator and licence.
  - **News** – Google News, Bing News and Yahoo News searches plus every news website's own feed (100+), only stories that are really about the search; Unitex News, GDELT, Hacker News, plus Bing News, Google News, Brave News and Mojeek News with the helper. Newest first, filter by outlet and country.
  - **Books** – Open Library, Google Books, Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive, Library of Congress, plus Google Books' search page and Amazon Books with the helper. "Free to read / borrow" badges, Read now, sort by year, reading list.
  - **Research papers** (under Books) – OpenAlex, Crossref, Wikipedia. "Free to read" filter, sort by citations or year, Copy citation.
  - **Maps** – places from OpenStreetMap and Photon with a live map beside the list; pick the map: OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, Satellite, Bing Maps, CyclOSM, Public transport or Humanitarian. Directions and saved places.
  - **Code** – GitHub, Stack Overflow, npm.
- **Results from every engine** – with the free [Unitex Helper](https://catancats.github.io/Unitex/install.html) extension, each search runs on Google, Bing, Brave, Mojeek, Yandex and SearXNG at once (ads are never shown: ad blocks, ad-click links and results labelled Ad or Sponsored are dropped) (and their image, video, news and shopping pages, Yandex Images, Amazon and eBay) (only engines with their own index; resellers like Ecosia, Yahoo and DuckDuckGo's web results aren't used), in your own browser and with your own cookies. Results are merged and ranked by how many engines agree, and each shows its website and "found by Bing · Yahoo · …". (Browsers don't let a website read other sites, so this one part has to be an extension: three short files in `extension/`. `test.html` shows what your browser allows with and without it.)
- **Works like the big engines** – suggestions while you type (Google and Bing through Unitex Helper, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia) plus your recent searches, with arrow keys and Enter; results must be about the search (small indexes need every word, big engines are trusted more, small typos still match), at most two results from one website near the top, search words in bold, a breadcrumb for each address, "Did you mean …?" and Related searches. Images, videos and books from the open collections need a search word in the title; cards made from engine results must match too. Code in `quality.js`.
- **Fast** – results appear as each source answers (the slowest never holds up the rest), the AI overview starts as soon as there's enough to read, every source's answer is kept for 2 minutes so going back to a tab is instant, the next page is fetched ahead when Show more comes near the screen, and the home page reads the other news sections in the background (and when you point at a tab).
- **Show more, forever** – every list (results, product and job cards, images, videos, news, books, papers, code) shows a first batch, then **Show more** reveals more and asks every source that can page for its next page, until none has anything new.
- **Sources panel** – the "⚙ Sources" button (the old "Searched" bar, now hidden until you open it) shows every engine, store and source with what it returned or why it failed. Click any one to switch it off or on, or switch off sites for the current filter; the choice is remembered. The country is picked there too.
- **Jobs from the job sites** – job cards come straight from Remotive, RemoteOK, Himalayas, Jobicy and Arbeitnow (read directly, no extension needed), and with Unitex Helper from SEEK, LinkedIn, Indeed and Jora for your country, plus job pages the engines find on the other job sites. The Job sites row shows each site and how many jobs it returned. Code in `jobs.js`.
- **Card pages laid out like the listing sites** – Houses, Jobs, Cars, Products, Second-hand, Recipes and Movies & TV have a filter panel on the left with every option (where, buy/rent, type, bedrooms, price, work type, make/model…, filled in from what you typed) and the search engines and websites; the results have a headline ("12 homes for rent in Brunswick VIC"), Sort, a List/Grid switch and Your list. List view is like realestate.com.au and SEEK: big photo (or company logo) on the left, price, address and facts on the right.
- **Fill-in search forms** – Houses (buy/rent/share, where, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking, price range, must-haves), Jobs (title, where, work type, experience, salary, remote), Cars (new/used, make, model, body, fuel, transmission, year, price, km, where), Products, Second-hand, Reviews, Recipes (ingredients, meal, cuisine, diet, time), Movies & TV, Travel (flights/stays/things to do, from, to, dates, travellers), Events, Learn and Open source each get a short form above the results. Its answers become the search and set the card filters, and are remembered per topic (`TOPIC_FORMS` in `modes.js`).
- **Modes open by themselves** – Jobs, Houses, Products, Cars, Second-hand, Recipes and Movies & TV open straight into their full-width mode with filters; Your list (saved items and compare) is a button above the cards, not a sidebar.
- **Every result says where it came from** (except when that's the site itself: an Amazon product doesn't say "found by Amazon") ("via Google", "via Reddit", "via Wikipedia · Marginalia" when several found it), with thumbnails where available. The **Images** tab pulls from many image libraries (see Tabs); a strip of images appears on All only when no topic is picked.
- **Sites for every filter** – Houses, Jobs, Cars, Second-hand, Shopping, Travel, Reviews, Recipes, Movies & TV, Events, Learn, Research and Open source each list the sites for your country (e.g. Houses in Australia: realestate.com.au, Domain, Allhomes, Homely, rent.com.au, Flatmates, view.com.au) with a direct search on each, plus one button that searches them all at once. The country is guessed from your browser and can be changed. Sites live in `topics.js`.
- **44 engines in 6 groups** – Web (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Yahoo, Brave, Startpage, Kagi, Qwant, Mojeek, …), AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI, Copilot, Grok, Le Chat), Opinions & communities (Reddit, Hacker News, Lobsters, Stack Overflow, AlternativeTo), Open source & code (GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, npm, PyPI, crates.io, …), Knowledge and Video & maps.
- **"Search for" row** under the search bar picks which Unitex tab a search opens (Shopping, Images, Maps…); the engine button in the search bar still picks any of the 45 engines – a big "Search everywhere with Unitex" button starts both rows; or type, then click any single engine or AI to send the query straight there. Click the engine in the search bar to pick from all of them.
- **Open-source alternatives** – type things like "alternative to Photoshop", "slack alternative", "foss google drive" or just "Notion", and Unitex shows well-known open-source replacements with their licenses, plus links to AlternativeTo, GitHub, what people say, and Claude/ChatGPT. The curated list lives in `alternatives.js`.
- **Bangs** – type `!g`, `!c` (Claude), `!gpt`, `!r`, `!gh`, `!w`… anywhere in a query to use that engine for one search. Each engine's bang is shown in the picker.
- **Live news from everywhere** – the home page reads the news live every time it opens, and again every 3 minutes: Google News, Bing News and Yahoo News for each section, plus that section's news websites' own feeds (BBC, Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, NYT, DW, ABC Australia, Times of India and about 100 more, listed in `scripts/feeds.json`), in Top, World, Business, Tech, Science, Sport, Culture and Open source. They're read through Unitex Helper (news feeds don't let other websites read them), or through a public feed relay (allorigins, codetabs) when the helper can't; GDELT, Reddit and Hacker News are read directly, with or without it. Stories are big tiles with the website, headline, summary, picture and "found by Google News · Bing News · Yahoo News"; a story found in several places is shown once. Every story links to **What people think** and **Other coverage**. Code in `livenews.js`.
- Light/dark theme, `/` to focus search, works on phones.
- **Default search engine** – add `https://catancats.github.io/Unitex/?q=%s` as a custom search engine in your browser (`%s` is where the browser puts your query).

Everything runs in your browser; queries go straight to the engine you pick.

## Hosting

GitHub Pages serves the `main` branch directly.

## How the news works

Nothing is pre-built: `livenews.js` reads the feeds in your browser when the page opens. To add a news website, add its RSS/Atom feed to `scripts/feeds.json` (and its host to the extension's `host_permissions` and `ALLOWED_HOSTS`).

## Adding an engine

Add an entry to `ENGINE_GROUPS` in `engines.js`, using `{q}` where the query goes.
