# Unitex

- Always commit and push directly to `main`. Never create or use other branches.
- The site is plain static files served by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://catancats.github.io/Unitex/: `index.html` + `app.js` + `livenews.js` (home), `search.html` + `search.js` (Unitex results; also loads `livenews.js` for the News tab's feed searches), shared `core.js`, `quality.js` (suggestions, relevance ranking, highlighting), `listings.js` (reads realestate.com.au / domain.com.au directly), `jobs.js` (job boards read directly, and SEEK/LinkedIn/Indeed/Jora through the helper), `sitereaders.js` (houses/cars/events/recipes/IMDb read from each section's companies through the helper; `SECTION_SITES`), `modes.js` (topic cards, modes, the Shopping area, shortlist), `ai.js` (AI overview and AI tab: Google AI Overview by default via the helper, Bing/Brave, Puter online models, the person's own Claude/OpenAI/Gemini keys, or on-computer models; settings and the provider list are in `core.js`), `tabs.js` (video player, image viewer, books, papers, maps with a map picker, news filters); tabs and their sub-filters are `TABS` in `search.js`; sources take `run(q, { page })` and set `paged: true` if they can fetch further pages (used by Show more); stores for Shopping are `STORE_SOURCES`/`STORES` in `helper.js` (new store hosts must also go in the extension's `host_permissions` and `ALLOWED_HOSTS`), data in `engines.js`, `topics.js`, `alternatives.js`, styles in `style.css`.
- Pages caches files for 10 minutes, so every `<script>`/`<link>` carries `?v=N`. When you change any JS or CSS, bump `N` in both `index.html` and `search.html`, or browsers mix old and new files and the page breaks.
- Home news is live: `livenews.js` reads Google News, Bing News, Yahoo News and the news websites in `scripts/feeds.json` in the browser (through the helper), plus GDELT/Reddit/Hacker News directly. There is no pre-built news list. A new feed host must also go in the extension's `host_permissions` and `ALLOWED_HOSTS`.
- `extension/` is the Unitex Helper browser extension (loads engine result pages for the site). `helper.js` is the page side and parses each engine's HTML. After changing anything in `extension/`, rebuild `unitex-helper.zip` with the three files at the zip's top level (no folder inside: Windows "Extract All" already makes a `unitex-helper` folder) and bump `version` in `extension/manifest.json` and `HELPER_LATEST` (plus a line in `HELPER_CHANGES`) in `version.js`, so pages tell people with an older helper to update.
